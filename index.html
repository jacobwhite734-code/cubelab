F2L learning roadmap

Start with pair recognition, then learn to pair and insert without disturbing solved slots. Use these guided stages in any order.

Every practice slot includes a guided drill and a short three-question checkpoint. Knowledge checks evaluate concepts, not physical execution.

Mastery rule: at least 5 attempts, at least 4 correct among the latest 5, and at least one correct timed attempt. Reviews are scheduled locally after 1, 3, 7, or 14 days based on your recorded results. Browser storage can be cleared; use Export to back up your progress.
